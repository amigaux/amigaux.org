## Install: Z3660 SD card

A3000, A4000 or A4000T with a Z3660 and a real 68060 or 68LC060. The system is one disk image, kept as a file
on the Z3660's SD card. Tested on real hardware: the 68060 image on an A4000T with a real 68060,
Kickstart 47.115 and firmware CDEB9618, from its first boot to a clean halt.

Two ways to set up the card:

- **[The SD card kit](#kit)**: everything a card needs in one download, laid out for its two
  partitions. For a new card, or to start afresh.
- **[Onto a card you already use](#card)**: the firmware file and the 68060 image, copied onto a
  card that already runs a Z3660.

Either way, bring the Kickstart 3.x ROM image for your machine (A3000 or A4000), from your own
ROMs: 524288 bytes. Tested: Kickstart 3.2.3 on an A4000, and the A4000T's 47.115.

### The SD card kit {#kit}

[`amix-1.1.2-z3660-sd-kit.zip`](../downloads/index.md#kit) holds `fat/` for the card's first
partition and `exfat/` for its second: the firmware CDEB9618 with upstream's stock start-up
loader, the settings and presets, and the disks, the ready installed 68060 system among them. Its
`exfat/README.txt` is the card's own guide. On a PC:

1. Take a microSD card of 8 GB or more with the Z3660's two partitions, FAT32 first and exFAT
   second, as [upstream's guide](https://github.com/shanshe/Z3660/blob/main/z3660-zturn_SD_content/README.md)
   describes. A card that already runs a Z3660 has them. The `exfat/` files take 4.9 GB.
2. Three of the Z3660 project's own files are not in the kit. A card that already runs a Z3660
   has them; otherwise download them from
   [upstream's exFAT folder](https://github.com/shanshe/Z3660/tree/main/z3660-zturn_SD_content/DATA%20Second%20Partition%20(exFat))
   and put them on the exFAT partition at exactly these paths:
    - `z3660_scsi.rom` (CRC32 `19712B63`): every preset needs it; without it no disk boots;
    - `kicks/kick060_scsi.rom` (CRC32 `12291596`): every preset needs it. Upstream calls it
      `kicks/kick060_z3660scsi.rom`: rename it;
    - `kicks/kick060.rom` (CRC32 `4C22FC9B`): no preset uses it; the boot menu offers it.
3. Copy the contents of `fat/` to the FAT32 partition and of `exfat/` to the exFAT partition,
   replacing the files of the same name.
4. Copy your Kickstart ROM image to `kicks/kickstart.rom` on the exFAT partition, with that exact
   name.
5. Put the card in the Z3660 and switch on. Preset 0, the default, boots the ready installed
   system.

The kit's other presets: 1 installs from the [install CD](cd.md) onto an empty 1 GiB disk at
unit 6, 2 installs over the [network](network.md) onto it, 3 boots that disk after an install.
Presets 6 and 7, for a system on a disk behind an A4092, are not yet tested; 4 and 5 are unused.
An installer halts the system when it is done: wait 10 seconds, then choose preset 3 for the next
power-on. Every copy of the kit has the same Ethernet address: see [below](#mac).

### Onto a card you already use {#card}

You need the [68060 image](../downloads/index.md#k46), unpacked. We recommend the Z3660 firmware
CDEB9618 from the [1.1 release](../downloads/index.md#z3660); step 1 puts it on the card.

#### 1. Firmware {#firmware}

1.1 also runs on the stock Z3660 firmware, 1.03 BETA 21 (tested on an A4000T with a real 68060).
CDEB9618 adds fixes the stock firmware lacks:

- a rare stop at start-up in which the machine resets itself. With a real 68040 or 68060, a start
  from power-off can still stop at a black screen now and then, a few times in a hundred: switch
  off and on again. A firmware fix for this is in preparation;
- disk transfers that could, rarely, go to the wrong block or leave stale data in the 68060's
  cache;
- network stalls: the Ethernet could stop for a while under load;
- uploads of files over 2 GiB through the card's web page.

On the stock firmware, copy large images with a card reader rather than through the card's web
page.

Both firmwares run the Z3660's Ethernet at 100 Mbit. CDEB9618 can also offer gigabit, with
`eth_adv1000 YES` in `z3660cfg.txt`, but a real 68060 can lose data at that speed; the stock
firmware has no gigabit.

To install CDEB9618, on a PC, with the Z3660's microSD card:

1. Optional, for an easy way back: if the first (FAT32) partition has no `FAILSAFE.bin`, copy
   `BOOT.BIN` to `FAILSAFE.bin` there.
2. Copy the firmware file to that partition as `Z3660.bin`, replacing a `Z3660.bin` that is
   already there; keep a copy of the old one on your PC to go back to. Leave `BOOT.BIN` as it is:
   the stock `BOOT.BIN` starts `Z3660.bin` (tested with the stock 1.03 BETA 21 `BOOT.BIN`).

To check the copy, compare its CRC32 with `CDEB9618`, with any CRC32 tool or in the firmware's
menu (`C` during the countdown at power-on, then `E`, then `CRC 0:/Z3660.bin`). When it runs, the
firmware's [console](../docs/serial-console.md#the-z3660s-own-console) says
`[SD Init] Done (Z3660.BIN)`. Your `z3660cfg.txt` keeps working.

#### 2. Kickstart

Copy your ROM image to `kicks/kickstart.rom` on the card's exFAT partition. On a real 68060 the
machine still starts from the ROM in its motherboard sockets, so the image must be the same
family: an A4000 Kickstart for an A4000 with its own ROMs.

#### 3. Copy the image

Copy `amix-1.1.2-k46-4g.img` into `hdf/` on the exFAT partition. Any file name works. 1.0 images
can stay beside it.

#### 4. Point a preset at it

The system must be SCSI unit 6. In `z3660cfg.txt`, name the image in a free slot and check the
two Kickstart lines, for example:

```
hdf4 hdf/amix-1.1.2-k46-4g.img
kickstart1 kicks/kickstart.rom
ext_kickstart2 kicks/kick060_scsi.rom
```

In the preset you boot, map that slot to unit 6, with these lines (the kit's preset 0 has them):

```
bootmode CPU
scsiboot YES
cpu_ram YES
kickstart 1
ext_kickstart 2
amix_mode NO
scsi6 4
```

`amix_mode YES` would stop the image from booting.

- Map only one bootable Amix disk in the preset you boot.
- The card has 20 image slots, `hdf0` to `hdf19`; a line for `hdf20` or above is ignored.

Then choose that preset in `presets/preset.txt`.

### Two or more Z3660s on one network {#mac}

Every card reports the same Ethernet address until you change `mac_address` in `z3660cfg.txt`.
Give each card its own, for example `02:00:00:00:00:02`, `…:03`: keep the leading `02` and use
digits only, as the firmware misreads A–F.

### First boot

Power on. The system asks its questions once: [first boot](../docs/first-boot.md). Then log in as
`root` with the password you gave it.

### If it goes wrong

- **It stops after loading the Kickstart:** this happens rarely. Switch off and on.
- **AmigaOS starts instead:** hold both mouse buttons at power-on, Boot Options, `UNIX_Boot`.
- **Back to the firmware you had:** copy your earlier `Z3660.bin` back over it on the FAT
  partition.
- **Back to the stock firmware:** delete `Z3660.bin` on the FAT partition. Full stock: copy
  `FAILSAFE.bin` over `BOOT.BIN`, then delete `Z3660.bin`.

**Switching off:** halt first, then wait 10 seconds and switch off: [starting and stopping
safely](../docs/start-stop.md). Never `reboot` or `shutdown -i6` on a real 68060: the Z3660 cannot
reset the CPU, so always switch off and on.

Stuck? Report it in [%SUPPORT_REPO%](%SUPPORT_ISSUES%); see [Support](../about/index.md#support).
