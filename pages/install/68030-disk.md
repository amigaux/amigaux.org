## Install: 68030 disk

An A3000, with the system disk at SCSI id 6 on its own controller. The image boots only there; on
any other machine or controller, an A4000 with a 68030 card and an A4091 among them, use the
[install CD](cd.md).

<span class="tier t-emu">Supported · emulation</span> Tested in emulation only; not yet run on real
hardware.

### You need

- the [68030 image](../downloads/index.md#k30), [written](../downloads/write-image.md#k30) to a SCSI
  disk of 3600 MiB or more, or served by a SCSI emulator;
- the Kickstart ROM for your machine, from your own ROMs.

### Install

1. Put the disk at SCSI id 6 on the A3000's own SCSI.
2. Power on. Kickstart boots the disk by itself and the system asks its questions once:
   [first boot](../docs/first-boot.md). Then log in as `root` with the password you gave it.

**AmigaOS starts instead:** hold both mouse buttons at power-on, Boot Options, `UNIX_Boot`.
