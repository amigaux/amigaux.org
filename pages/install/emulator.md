## Install: emulator

Both images run in an emulator: the 68030 image in an emulated A3000, the 68060 image on an
emulated Z3660. The disk image is the emulated SCSI disk at id 6 (on the Z3660: unit 6).

- **Amiberry:** the amigaux build, not published yet.
- **WinUAE:** not tested with 1.1.

Bring your own Kickstart ROM image.

Then [first boot](../docs/first-boot.md).
