## Contribute

Amiga UNIX is built in the open, and help of every size is welcome.

- **Report a bug, ask, or suggest:** in [%SUPPORT_REPO%](%SUPPORT_ISSUES%), an issue for a bug,
  [Discussions](%SUPPORT_DISCUSSIONS%) for questions and ideas.
- **Your hardware:** run it and tell us what happened; the
  [hardware page](../hardware/index.md) says what to include.
- **Code, drivers, packages:** everything that builds the system is in
  [amigaux/amix](https://github.com/amigaux/amix). Its
  [contributing guide](https://github.com/amigaux/amix/blob/master/CONTRIBUTING.md) says how to send
  a change, the licence, what never goes in, and how to build and test.
- **Say hi:** see [How can I help?](../faq/index.md#how-can-i-help) in the FAQ.
